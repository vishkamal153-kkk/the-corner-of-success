About The Corner of Success

The Corner of Success is a career-exploration learning project designed to help students discover interests, find learning resources, and try hands-on challenges.

Created by: The Corner of Success project team - Vishwa, Ritwik, and Ishaan

Educational resources

The app links to these independent educational websites and discovery services:

  • Khan Academy — subject lessons and practice.
  • IXL — learning and skill-practice resources.
  • The Art Story — art history information.
  • YouTube — video-search links for learning topics.

Design & technology

The app is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Its optional web fonts, DM Sans and Outfit , are loaded from Google Fonts . The career icons and decorative symbols use standard emoji and text symbols.

Privacy & Local Accounts

This version of the app saves demo account information and exercise progress in this browser's local storage. It does not provide an online account system. Use a nickname, a made-up email, and a password you never use elsewhere. The app stores your name, demo email, salt, password digest, and progress locally; browser access can expose or change them, so the login screen protects no private content. Profiles do not sync between devices, and clearing browser data erases them.

The app does not upload profile fields or exercise progress to an account server. GitHub receives requests when you visit this hosted site. Google receives requests for web fonts, and external learning sites receive requests when you follow links. Those services apply their own privacy policies. Public source code is visible to everyone.

To erase demo profiles and progress, use your browser's site-data controls for this site.
